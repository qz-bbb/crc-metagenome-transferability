# Fixed analytical protocol

This is a portable adaptation of the completed four-definition prediction extension. It changes import/config/I/O boundaries, not the numerical question or rules. A retrospective code release cannot recreate preregistration timing.

The four definitions are raw_mean,raw_median,clr_mean,clr_median: CRC-minus-control group effect on raw percentage abundance or CLR, summarized by mean or median. Reference direction is the training association coefficient sign. Detection means any positive reported abundance. Effects within 1e-12 or unreported/undetected taxa are discordant, retained in the full study/group denominator; neither missing whole samples nor unresolved cross-database identities are imputed.

Every outer training fold recomputes prevalence>=0.05 and detection in>=6 studies, lexical retained-feature order, pseudocount equal to half the minimum positive training abundance, full-universe CLR, HC3 study-adjusted association and complete-family BH, within-study direction and inner study-removal evidence. Direction threshold is0.60; association and random-effects q<0.10; sign retention>=0.90; q retention>=0.80; I2<75. The sign-retention comparison retains tiny same-sign nonzero coefficients exactly as in the source. No external or outer-test labels enter selection.

The common panel size is min(18,the natural sizes of all eight DCP/ICP groups). ARP is association-ranked at that size; selected features sort by association q then full taxon string. No padding. Legitimately empty common panels are unavailable and must not receive complete-grid efficacy claims. CLR always uses every retained feature, including unselected ones. Scaling and fitting use training data only.

Elastic Net: penalty=elasticnet,solver=saga,l1_ratio=0.5,C=0.5,class_weight=balanced,max_iter=2000,random_state=42. Random Forest:500 trees,max_features=sqrt,max_depth=None,min_samples_leaf=2,bootstrap=True,class_weight=balanced_subsample,random_state=20260817,n_jobs=1. Convergence failure stops the action without tuning. Nine panels and two learners remain in every complete prediction/evaluation output.

Metrics are standard scikit-learn AUROC and average precision (AP,not trapezoidal PR area). Fixed-model conditional uncertainty uses seed20261011 and2000 class-stratified draws shared by all18 models. Sample order is sorted sample_uid; controls are sampled as one array, then CRC as another. Internal streams use SeedSequence([20261011,1,index_in_sorted_nine_studies]); external streams use SeedSequence([20261011,2,index_in_sorted_six_cohorts]). Each actual index array is hashed as little-endian int64 in C order. Identical predictions may reuse scores, but all model labels remain.

Equal-study internal macro and fixed primary-three external macro average replicate scores before quantiles. All41 prespecified contrasts per metric are preserved, including interactions and RF-minus-EN. The two EN ICP AUROC contrasts raw_mean-minus-raw_median and clr_median-minus-raw_median retain nominal97.5% intervals; external flags are descriptive protocol-focal labels,not fresh confirmatory endpoints. Intervals condition on current fitted models/cohorts,not training uncertainty,future-population uncertainty or equivalence.

Primary external cohorts: LiuNN_2022,YangY_2021,IIGM_TU. Secondary: YangJ_2020,NHSII,IIGM_CZ. All six had outcomes inspected previously. Full-training models are locked before blind prediction; labelled evaluation waits for all six blind grids. Adenomas are excluded. CZ uses59 CRC/46 controls,including eligible polyp controls; the 43 alias-only training identities remain a limitation.

Direction-context tables retain all800 coordinates, every natural/selected candidate group and all four definitions. Historical Welch CLR-mean q values are BH-adjusted over the full800 family and belong only to that inferential definition; they are not recomputed or transferred as evidence for the other scales. External direction is contextual evidence,not model reselection or causal validation.
