Figure 8. Equal-size predictive comparisons across four direction definitions. All panels contain 18 taxa. (A) Nested nine-study internal equal-study macro AUROC. (B) External equal-study macro AUROC across three primary cohorts; the other three external cohorts remain separately reported in the source data. Circles indicate elastic net (EN) and open triangles random forest (RF); horizontal intervals are 95% fixed-model conditional bootstrap intervals. (C) Two focal EN paired changes from raw-median ICP, at internal and external scopes, with nominal 97.5% conditional intervals. The external raw-mean lower bound is −0.00002164397240382981, so this interval includes zero. (D) All four ICP definitions versus ARP, for both learners at each scope, with paired 95% conditional intervals. Internal results use filled circles and solid intervals; external results use open squares and dashed intervals in C/D. Positive differences favor the first-named panel. ARP and CLR-mean DCP use identical selected panels; raw-mean ICP and CLR-mean ICP also use identical selected panels. These duplicates are not independent confirmations. The extension was designed after earlier external outcomes were known and is a post-outcome sensitivity analysis, not untouched confirmatory validation. Macro estimates weight studies equally and are not pooled sample-level estimates. Bootstrap intervals condition on fixed fitted models and omit model-development/refit and new-population uncertainty. The comparisons establish neither ICP superiority over ARP nor equivalence.
